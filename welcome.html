How It Works

Simple concept. Serious strategy.

01
You Get $60,000
Every week you get $60,000 to spend on any active NFL players. Star players cost more — that's the strategy.
02
Pick Your Team
Fill 9 roster slots: QB, 2 RBs, 3 WRs, TE, Flex, Defense. Stay under the cap. Submit your lineup before the first game kicks off.
03
Games Kick Off
Players lock when their individual game starts — not all at once. Sunday Night and Monday Night players stay available until their kickoff.
04
Watch It Unfold
Points update automatically as your players perform. Standings update live all day. Scores finalize Tuesday morning.
