How It Works

Simple concept. Serious strategy.

01
Get Your Cap
Every week you get $60,000 in salary cap. Player salaries are set based on projected fantasy points — the better the player, the higher the cost.
02
Build Your Lineup
Draft 9 players: 1 QB, 2 RBs, 3 WRs, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, 1 DEF. Mix stars and value plays to stay under cap. Submit before kickoff.
03
Players Lock
Players lock individually when their game kicks off. Pick late-game players strategically — you can still swap in unlocked players as the day unfolds.
04
Scores Update Live
Fantasy points calculate in real time using DraftKings scoring. Standings update automatically. Final scores lock Tuesday morning.
🏈
New Lineup Every Week
No season-long commitments to players. Bad week? Fresh start next Sunday.
💰
Salary Cap Strategy
$60,000 cap forces real decisions. You can't roster everyone — build smarter.
🔒
Player-Level Locks
Players lock at kickoff, not all at once. Sunday 1pm locks are different from MNF locks.
📡
Live Scoring
Scores update in real time during games. Watch the leaderboard shift play by play.
📊
Season-Long Standings
Cumulative scores tracked all season. Weekly high scores, streaks, and a playoff bracket.
🏆
Playoff Bracket
Top performers make the playoffs. Season culminates in a champion crowned Week 18.
